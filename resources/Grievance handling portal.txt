GridOps — Product Definition
GridOps = Utility Operations & Field Intelligence Platform
Core promise:
Know what is pending, who has it, when it is due, and what needs attention.
The four modules you identified fit naturally into that promise:
Grievance & DAK workflow
Meter Challenge tracking
Work / deadline management
GIS Survey & field mapping
AI comes later as an intelligence layer.
